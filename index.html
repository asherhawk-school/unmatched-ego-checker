How it works
  • Brag about yourself in full sentences. The scanner decides how impressive each one is. The exact scoring is secret.
  • Every brag word only impresses once per match. Find new ways to show off.
  • No repeats: a sentence that you or your rival already said is rejected.
  • One sentence every 2 seconds. Punctuation and caps don't earn extra points.
  • The scanner understands word forms, typos, stretched words like “sooo”, words like “extremely”, and “not”. Real sentences beat lists of words, and praising your rival backfires.
  • Roast your rival (aim an insult at "you" or their name) to knock their score down.
  • Humble talk costs you points, and made-up gibberish words cost 500 each.
  • Ratings start at 1,000 and climb slowly: up to 20 points a match for your first 10 games, then up to 12. Only your first 3 matches against the same rival each day count.
  • You can't be matched with accounts that were used on your own device, and wins only count against active players who aren't brand new.
  • Click any player's name to see their profile.
