How it works
  • A small AI model reads each sentence and rates how egotistical it sounds, from humble to unmatched. No single word is worth points on its own: the meaning of the whole sentence counts.
  • No repeats, judged by meaning: saying an earlier brag in new words scores half, and near-copies of anything said this match are rejected.
  • One sentence every 2 seconds. Very short sentences count less, and made-up gibberish words cost 500 each.
  • Roast your rival (talk to "you" or use their name). The AI judges how harsh it is, and harsher roasts take more of their points.
  • Humble talk and complimenting other people score below zero.
  • Ratings start at 1,000 and climb slowly: up to 20 points a match for your first 10 games, then up to 12. Only your first 3 matches against the same rival each day count.
  • You can't be matched with accounts that were used on your own device, and wins only count against active players who aren't brand new.
  • 2v2: you and a teammate face another pair. Your team score is both of your points added up. Roasts hit the rival you name, or the enemy with the most points if you don't name one.
  • Click any player's name to see their profile.
