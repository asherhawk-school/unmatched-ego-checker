How scoring works
  • Talking about yourself (I, me, my, myself) earns points in every sentence.
  • Brag words like genius, legend, flawless and GOAT score big, but each one only counts once per match. Use it again and it scores nothing.
  • No repeats: a sentence that you or your rival already said is rejected. Rewording with the same words doesn't count either.
  • Bragging about yourself in one sentence doubles it. Each "!" adds 0.4×, up to 3×.
  • Claiming you're better than Dempsey pays +8,000 once per match. Praising Dempsey costs 4,000.
  • Humble words (sorry, maybe, thanks, we, team) cost points.
  • Roast your rival: aim an insult at them ("you", "your", or their name) and they lose points. Each insult only works once per match, and "!" makes it hit harder.
  • Ratings start at 1,000. Beating a higher-rated player earns more.
